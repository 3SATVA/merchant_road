How to Play

Trade. Buy goods in a town and sell them in another. The farther you carry them, the more they pay (about +20% per town-length). Selling where you bought loses a little. Prices drift over time.

Goods: Apple $5 · Potato $10 · Wood $30 · Coal $100 · Iron $500 · Gold $2000. Pricier goods are sold further east.

Haulers: Backpack 5 → Wheelbarrow 12 → Small Wagon 25 → Big Wagon 50. Bigger is slower.

Danger. Plains: slimes, goblins. Snow: golems, goblins, deep snow, blizzards. Mountains: golems, witches, slopes, rockfall. Volcano: witches, lava golems, heat, lava pits.

Risk. Hits can knock goods off your hauler (pick them back up). Goblins steal one item at a time and slow down while carrying them — catch and kill them to get them back. If you fall, you lose all cargo (half of what you paid for it is refunded) but keep money and gear, and wake at the last town.

Carts. Press C (🛒) to unhitch your cart and park it anywhere. You walk faster without it, but only your backpack (5) comes along, and you can only sell what you carry. Walk back and press C to hitch up again. Goblins may raid a cart left alone.

Gear. Press I (🎒) for your inventory: goods, cart and gear. Tap gear to equip. Monsters sometimes drop things: slimes apples (25%), goblins daggers, each 10%. Golems drop Sentient Stone (30%), witches Magic Essence (30%), lava golems Dried Lava (10%). Materials don't use cargo space. Spare copies of gear are sold on the spot.

Forge. Frostholm, Stonepeak and Emberfall have a Forge (anvil sign). Flame Sword or Fire Staff (homing fireballs): 2 essence, 2 iron, 1 dried lava each (iron comes from your cargo). Witch Hat: 2 essence (+25% ranged). Witch Robes: 3 essence (+15% ranged, 8% less damage). Stone Chestplate: 3 stone (20% less damage). Stone Helm: 2 stone (10% less damage). Witch Boots: 2 essence (+10% ranged, +8% speed). Stone Boots: 2 stone (6% less damage, half knockback). One item per slot.

Sets. Wear all 3 witch pieces: twin shots (each 75% damage). All 3 stone pieces: +30 max HP, no knockback, and hits never knock goods off your hauler. Smiths in Frostholm and Emberfall sell the Leaf Staff ($600): homing leaves (gentler turn than fire), 9 damage.

Barber. Wake Ridge, Pinehollow and Emberfall have a Barber (striped pole): change skin, body and hair for free. Your Top Hat goes on your head; take it off to show your hair. One hat at a time: wearing a Witch Hat puts the Top Hat back in your bag.

Witch. Vex, a young witch with a knack for enchanting, parks her wagon (purple tarp) somewhere on the road and moves on every ~10 minutes. Get close and it shows on your map (purple). Press E at her wagon, tap a weapon or armor piece (or hold it briefly and drag) into the slot and press Enchant for 3 Magic Essence: it replaces the item's enchants with 1–3 random ones, levels I–III. Reroll as often as you like. Weapons: Sharp, Critical, Knockback, Burning, Freezing, Venom, Leeching. Armor: Guarded, Swift, Hardy, Thorns, Regen, Leeching. Curses (about 1 in 6) are high risk, high reward: Bloodthirst, Berserk, Volatile, Heavy, Greedy.

Horses. Bog Town, Pinehollow and Stonepeak have a Stable (horseshoe sign): a horse costs $1,500, no upkeep, and comes with a Wooden Lance. Press R (🐴) to ride or get off; if your horse is somewhere else it gallops over to you (not inside caves). Riding is 1.5× faster and pulls your cart (a loaded cart slows it a bit). On horseback you fight only with your lance: tap attack to lower it, and the faster you gallop into a foe, the harder it hits. Hits never knock you off, but a rider is an easy target: you take 30% more damage and each hit knocks you out of your gallop. Golems don't budge: a charge or a golem punch shoves you back (BONK), so circle round and charge again. Goblins can't snatch goods while you gallop (right after a hit they can). Horses wait outside caves. Iron Lance at the Stable ($900); Magma Lance at the Forge (3 stone, 1 dried lava, 1 iron). Vex enchants lances too.

Ashen Wastes. Past Emberfall: no towns, just raiders (they steal!), cinder slimes, scrap golems and ash storms. At the far end, build an airship from the scrap heap: 150 Wood, 50 Apples, 50 Gold (press E; a cart parked there counts). Deposits are never lost. You need a horse to fly it to World 2, the Skylands: your horse grows wings there (tap jump in the air to flap), carts stay parked by the airship and each world has its own money (you start the Skylands with $100) and its own goods: World 1 goods wait in your wagon by the airship, sky goods wait in your airship's hold. Gear, materials, lances and your horse come with you. Skyhaven sells bigger airships; fly home from its dock.

Sky travel: on the winged horse, jump takes off. In the air the up/down arrows on the left change altitude (3 bands; high is fastest; keys W/S). The big right button leaves the airship at a dock (or leaps onto your horse mid-air, the airship flies home with its hold) and gets off the horse over land (key R). The winged horse is slow and only carries your backpack. Board your airship at its dock to haul a full hold to Sunspire; pegasi and sky slimes hit its hull, and a broken hull limps back to the dock losing some cargo (half refunded). Rain pushes you down, updrafts lift you.

Caves. Rare caves line the road (skulls = difficulty, harder further east). Carts wait outside. Jump ledges and lifts, dodge spikes, beat the monsters and open the chest for materials. A fall puts you back on the last ledge; get knocked out and you're dragged outside, nothing lost. Looted caves refill after a while.

Keys: A/D or ←/→ move · Space/W jump · J or click attack · E enter town/cave · C cart · R horse · I inventory · Q swap weapon · H potion · Esc pause.

Fire melts ice golems, ice cracks lava golems, hammers crush all golems.
