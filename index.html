How to Play

Trade. Buy goods in a town and sell them in another. The farther you carry them, the more they pay (about +20% per town-length). Selling where you bought loses a little. Prices drift over time.

Goods: Apple $5 · Potato $10 · Wood $30 · Coal $100 · Iron $500 · Gold $2000. Pricier goods are sold further east.

Haulers: Backpack 5 → Wheelbarrow 12 → Small Wagon 25 → Big Wagon 50. Bigger is slower.

Danger. Plains: slimes, goblins. Snow: golems, goblins, deep snow, blizzards. Mountains: golems, witches, slopes, rockfall. Volcano: witches, lava golems, heat, lava pits.

Risk. Hits can knock goods off your hauler (pick them back up). Goblins steal goods — kill them to get them back. If you fall, you lose all cargo but keep money and gear, and wake at the last town.

Keys: A/D or ←/→ move · Space/W jump · J or click attack · E enter town · Q swap weapon · H potion · Esc pause.

Fire melts ice golems, ice cracks lava golems, hammers crush all golems.
